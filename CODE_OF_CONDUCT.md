# Community code of conduct

PC to iPhone Share welcomes contributors of different backgrounds and experience levels. Treat other participants with respect. Use clear, constructive feedback, make room for questions, and respect privacy.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, publishing private information, and repeated hostile behavior are not acceptable. Maintainers may remove inappropriate content or restrict participation to keep the project useful and safe.

For threats, harassment, or exposed private information on GitHub, use [GitHub's private abuse-reporting flow](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/content-removal-policies/reporting-abuse-or-spam). A separate private maintainer conduct channel has not been designated; do not publish sensitive allegations or personal details in an issue. For software vulnerabilities use the private channel in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). The maintainer is [dibyapp](https://github.com/dibyapp).
