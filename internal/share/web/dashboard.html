First-time device setup

Your phone must trust this computer’s local certificate before it can verify HTTPS. Check the phone address and certificate fingerprint against this dashboard. Install only the profile from your own computer.

  1. Open the phone address in Safari.
  2. Download the certificate profile from the login screen’s setup link.
  3. Install it in Settings → General → VPN & Device Management.
  4. Enable it in Settings → General → About → Certificate Trust Settings.
  5. Reopen Safari and enter your share password.

Android: download the public certificate and use your device’s security settings to install a CA certificate. Labels vary by manufacturer.
